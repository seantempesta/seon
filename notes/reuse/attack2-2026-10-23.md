# Second attack on the reuse design (notes/reuse/design.md at a08ef67b), 2026-10-23

Verdict: unsound as written, sound with fixes. The foundation needs changing in two places:
1. The release binary direct-links (bin/build:202; jolt README:532), so callers keep the callee bodies they were compiled against. A Merkle over the current loaded record treats every edge as late-bound: after `load-form!` replaces `seon.print/line`, `print-ai` still runs the old one, and its stale text is stored under the new id, where the next binary hits it. Fix: record each var's ref ids as they were at compile time; when a load changes a var, everything that reaches it gets the nonce until it is reloaded.
2. A data def is identified by its text, not its value (`(def schema-forms (baked-schema-forms))`, config.clj:71-85; by-identity and entity-schemas, render.clj:61-68, 200-204). Fix: identify a non-function root by its canonical EDN.
Also open:
3. Both faces' render vars and nested data-chosen renders (render.clj:84-95, 124-138, 317, 321).
4. REPL load-file and eval are credited to the outer form's text.
5. clojure.core and library vars: the library pins aren't in the key, and the Jolt fingerprint is a 32-bit FNV.
6. encode sorts maps and sets, but the printer keeps iteration order (select.clj:40,114-130).
7. Refused bindings fall back to "".
8. encode loops on (repeat nil).
9. Component digests are 64-bit.
10. Mutable roots defeat identity checks.
11. The bake's order relative to arm-all! is unspecified.
12. The key's build cost isn't measured.
13. Eviction thrash, and unbounded disk on the :derive branch.
