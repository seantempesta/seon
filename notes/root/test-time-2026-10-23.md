# Where bin/prove's 176 s goes (2026-10-23)

The question: `bin/prove` on jolt/process-tests (b30edac) takes 176 s cold, against root's estimate of about 10 s. Short answer: **actual test work is about 31 s of wall time. The other ~145 s is a seon binary build nobody needed (46 s), a Jolt AOT-cache miss that costs ~11 s in every Jolt process (3 of them are serial on the critical path), the plan re-analysing all of src+test under contracts (16 s), and one 47 s property test on a shard dealt by count rather than by time.**

Sources. The run's own files: `next-process-tests/target/prove/*` (build.log, shard-N.log and their mtimes, captured at 07:14, before a later run overwrote them), `target/check/20261023T071402/*` (step mtimes), and the per-test `:ms` stored at each digest in `../test-results/` (`seon.proofs/run-tests!` records it; 351 results written by the four shards between 07:13:19 and 07:14:02). Measurements: single probe processes under `nice -n 15` in my own worktree `next-test-cost` (at b30edac), on a loaded machine, so measured wall times are high rather than low. No suite or shard was run.
