# RT-10 merge! carries its proofs' green evidence onto the target

Ledger §3.28 RT-10 (group M; inside MA-11). Lane: Opus (with MA-11).

## What becomes true
The merge transaction onto the target carries, beside the program rows, norms and the closed request (MA-11, TX-6a),
the member results of the request's proofs from the run on the merge branch, so `seon.test/verified?` on the target
answers true for each proof at the merged program, the request's proofs are green, and the same transaction retracts
root's assignment of it (RT-3). The other reaching tests' results stay on the merge branch (they are evidence of the
merge, consumed by the score) unless MA-11 already carries them.

## Functions
- CHANGE MA-11's merge transaction builder (`seon.cluster.source`/`my.program`): add the proofs' member rows (their
  identities and reach digests, as recorded by the run) and the assignment retraction.

## Deleted
Nothing.

## Proof
`seon.merge-policy-test/a-merged-request-is-green-on-the-target` (new): after `merge!` commits, every proof of the
request is `verified?` on the target and root no longer holds the request.

## Cost
The proofs' member rows (a few datoms per proof) ride the one merge transaction.

## Dependencies
MA-11, RT-3, RT-9. Lands in MA-11's commit or right after it.
