# S4-07a Token counts on the attempt row

Ledger row S4-07a (group D). Moves the calibration's character count off the capture (deleted in S4-07b3) onto the
attempt row the provider's own `prompt_tokens` already sits on, and moves `message-custody` out of the code S4-07b3
deletes. Lane: Sonnet.

## Files
`resources/seon/schemas/seon.ai.attempt.edn`, `src/seon/turn.clj` (attempt write), `src/seon/cluster/prompt.clj`
(`calibration-for`), `src/seon/context.clj` (move only), `test/seon/cluster/prompt_test.clj`.

## Functions
- SCHEMA: add `[:seon.ai.tokens/characters {:optional true} :seon.ai.tokens/characters]` to the attempt entity in
  `seon.ai.attempt.edn` (`:seon.ai.tokens/characters` is `[:int {:min 0}]`, `seon.ai.tokens.edn:19`).
- CHANGE the attempt write in `seon.turn` (the map transacted under tempid `"attempt"`, `src/seon/turn.clj:4250-4273`):
  add `:seon.ai.tokens/characters (count prompt-text)` where `prompt-text` is the exact text placed in
  `:seon.ai/prompt` (`:4646`), plus the system message's length once S4-02b1 has landed.
- CHANGE `seon.cluster.prompt/calibration-for` (`src/seon/cluster/prompt.clj:74-104`, contract unchanged): replace
  the two capture clauses (`[?capture :seon.context.capture/run ?run] [?capture :seon.ai.tokens/characters ?characters]`)
  with `[?attempt :seon.ai.tokens/characters ?characters]`.
- STOP writing `:seon.ai.tokens/characters` in `seon.context/capture-tx` (`src/seon/context.clj:434-435`).
- MOVE `seon.context/message-custody` (`src/seon/context.clj:258`) unchanged to just below the `ns` form (outside the
  capture/selection region S4-07b3 deletes).

## Proof
Existing `seon.cluster.prompt-test/calibration-uses-the-agents-recent-attempts-and-config-prior`
(`test/seon/cluster/prompt_test.clj:382`) stays green after its fixture writes characters on the attempt instead of
the capture (convert its fixture, not its assertions).
