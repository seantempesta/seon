adoption were not reproduced; the empty-store boot is a different path). Model: Sonnet. No source change.

## Work

1. Copy default's store (`data/store` and its siblings the operator names) into a throwaway root under the scratchpad;
   never touch default.
2. Start that root on default's committed HEAD with `JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS="-Xlog:gc* -XX:StartFlightRecording=settings=profile,stackdepth=256"`
   (`bin/seon --root R start`), wait for ready, record ready-ms and adoption ms from status.
3. From the JFR recording: allocation and CPU by owner for the adoption window; in particular whether a restart
   re-analyzes unchanged files (`seon.fn.analyzer/analyze`, `seon.fn/analyzed-files` calls and their inputs) and
   whether `build-projection` misses on an unchanged population (PB-16).
4. Repeat once to separate cold class loading from work.
