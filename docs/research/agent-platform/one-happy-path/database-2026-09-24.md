# One happy path: the database layer (2026-09-24)

Area: `seon.db`, the `seon.schema.datahike` bridge, caches keyed on commits, `vary-meta` stamps,
`seon.cluster.{store,registry,export}`, `seon.blob`, `seon.reconcile`, and anything that reimplements Datahike.
HEAD is `db5b68505`. The Datahike fork is `reference-code/datahike` at `c79cd03a`. The working tree has
uncommitted edits in `db.clj` (+16 lines) and in the fork's `query/execute.cljc`, both from other lanes, so
line numbers are cited from the working tree. Spans are clj-kondo `var-definitions` rows. This file
extends [shrink-db](../shrink-db-2026-09-23.md) (rows A–U) and re-verifies it at HEAD without repeating it.

  "What the dependency lacks" below, not a row.

## Totals

- **About 1,420 lines deletable in this area**: rows 1–14. About 290 of them sit in rows 9–11 and are UNVERIFIED.
- **A further ~600 lines after the fork's comparator fix**: the codec, `schema/datahike.clj:299-565` 266 plus
  `db.clj`'s remaining codec, with `mark-encoded`, the last `vary-meta` stamp at `schema/datahike.clj:327`.

**Three highest-value slices:**

1. **Row 2 plus row 13: delete the projection stamp.** This removes the 477 ms constructor on publication,
   the 80× `db` overhead and 11 stamp writer and reader sites. `carried-projection` is already the answer, and
   probe P4 is true today.
2. **Row 1: read currency as revision equality.** −420 lines. It removes the sub-queries and whole-result
   hashing added to every captured read. Every agent turn's since-diff runs through it.
3. **Row 4 plus install `b29c2ac77`, then rows 6 and 7.** The profiled 245 s spent in `q` and
   `with-declarations` is the largest measured hot path in the JVM. Deleting the guard and arity duplicates is
   low risk on the same file.

## What the dependency genuinely lacks (fix it in our fork, not beside it)

- **A total comparator for `:db.type/any` values**: map replacement in `cmp-temporal-datoms-eavt-quick`, and
  `cmp-nil` equating unequal maps (`lane-a2-storage-retention-2026-09-22.md`). Fixing it deletes the whole EDN
  codec. The ruling rejects a partial patch.
- **A variable attribute bound with a free value** (`DH/query/execute.cljc:487`). The fix is in progress in the
  fork (`var-attr-bound-attrs`, uncommitted). Row 8 deletes the caller workaround once it lands.
- **A merge base / common ancestor over the commit graph.** `versioning.cljc` offers `branch-history` and
  `parent-commit-ids` only. Move `merge-base` there (row 14).
- **Store identity baked into every commit's stored config**, which forces `reidentify-at!` to rewrite the commit
  graph (`DH/connector.cljc:165`). A fork option to take the identity from connect-time config would delete
  row 10's clone half. UNVERIFIED; this needs a fork probe.
