## Verdict

Revise before implementation. The strongest deletions are the EDN mirror and fitting loop, Lucene machinery without production queries, duplicated task lifecycle and ambient context reconstruction. The first pass nevertheless preserves repeated schema/occurrence work, broadens contracts under D12, invents a forbidden task wake attribute, and stages retirements that cannot keep HEAD loadable. Keep the ambitious size target, but make it conditional on the explicit task, result and cross-owner guarantees above; the two live reads verify a healthy PREPL and correct the byte claim, not the unimplemented flow.
