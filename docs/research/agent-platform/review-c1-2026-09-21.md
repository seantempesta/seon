## Verdict

Revise before implementation. The owner’s feature fits the existing wrapper, definition facts, settlement, test observation and task writer, but C1 presently adds false self-time inference, destructive counter races and an invalid version identity schema, while omitting important completion paths. Inclusive cumulative evidence with explicit scope and uncertainty is the smaller credible starting point; share B4’s observation and B3’s routing, remove the unsupported threshold and history guarantees, and settle lifetime/persistence/task criteria with the listed probes. The 240-line budget is neither a demonstrated minimum nor evidence of correctness; the missing guarantees must be resolved before counting lines.
