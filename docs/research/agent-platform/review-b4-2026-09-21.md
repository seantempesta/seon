## Verdict

Revise before implementation. The worker/process deletion and reuse of the hosting program are well grounded, and broad static selection is independently verified. The proposed replacement currently sacrifices evidence needed for correct reuse, destructive hosting and actual termination, while several commits cannot load. Make the flow depend on each execution's own basis, carry coherent fixture custody, state the amended E2 exclusion guarantee explicitly, and convert callers with their owners. The ≤3,300 target is a useful stretch target, not yet a supported estimate; lowering the count must follow deletion of a proven redundant mechanism, never deletion of its still-required guarantee.
