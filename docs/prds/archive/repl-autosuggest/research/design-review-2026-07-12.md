# REPL autosuggest — design review synthesis (2026-07-12)

Inputs: 4 reader dossiers (provider-routing, typeahead-overlap, reconcile-internals, ctx-profiles, needle-envelope) + 3 judges (duplication, will-it-work, integration). All three judges: **right-track-with-changes**. One judge live-verified claims against the running stores; line refs below are theirs.

---

## 1. Verdict

**The approach is right at the architecture level and wrong in its evidentiary foundation.** Every structural decision sits at a sanctioned seam: one renderer with a profile (no second producer), an advisory ctx section on the driver's native emission path (the correct side of the glyph-channel lesson), the existing `my.plan` surface as the plan target (no invented features, per owner #1), the proven src-diffusion wire contract, measurement inside the three sanctioned surfaces. None of that changes. But the design's three load-bearing *empirical* claims are unmeasured or measured-false: the mined-turns data source is **~224 usable turns total** (live-verified: all in acme; default's history was wiped by cluster reset — its 5,199 blobs are unminable orphans under the byte-exact rule; 4 `transact!` / 5 `reconcile!` examples against needle's own ≥120-per-kind guideline); the "≤~700 BPE" projection figure is a seon `chars/4` estimate, not needle-BPE (Clojure inflates ~1.5–2.5× under its English+JSON tokenizer, plausibly blowing the 1024/512 envelope); and the "retrain tokenizer if needed" fallback is illusory (embeddings tied to the output projection, `architecture.py:331,363` — retokenize ≡ pretrain ≡ 200B tokens ≡ infeasible locally). **The single most important correction: invert the order of work — run the no-training kill tests (tokenizer census, copy fidelity, projection signal-ceiling, oracle-injection uptake) BEFORE the B1 MLX port and any training spend, and re-found the data recipe as synthetic/gold-primary with the 224 mined turns as the held-out real-distribution eval set.** The owner's under-research concern was warranted — not about the seams, which are sound, but about the specifics: the tokenizer claim was asserted, the corpus was never counted, and three needle subsystems (contrastive head, constrained decoding, loss weighting) are oversold as portable when they are JSON-hard-wired.

## 2. What stands

- **ONE renderer, profile over `render-context`** (settled, correct). Verified live: the producer is single (`seon.agent.turn/render-prompt` is a thin delegate; web debug routes through it) and renders byte-identically over `db/as-of` values at the same t. The ban on a second renderer is the right call and matches the ONE-mechanism table's explicit "never a second block set."
- **Advisory ctx section, no selection protocol.** The frozen glyph channel's precise lesson supports this design: what died was the out-of-distribution *selection act* (0/129 organic glyph emissions, .019 auto-offer uptake); menu *text* as passive context earned its render (+.348 combined). Autosuggest acceptance = the driver writing ordinary Clojure it just read — its native emission path. Right side of the lane's own dichotomy.
- **Complement, not duplicate, of the typeahead lane.** Boundary in one sentence: the provider seam answers WHO generates the reply (`:typeahead` replaces the driver for a worker agent); the ctx-block seam answers WHAT the generator sees (`:suggest` advises any driver). They compose (a suggestion can render into a worker's context). Merging them would mint a third noun — don't.
- **Plan flagship uses ONLY the existing `my.plan` surface** (owner #1 honored). The reconcile-internals analysis confirms the mapping is genuine judgment work — identity resolution, delta-vs-whole-document shape choice, ordering→`::needs` inference — and even frontier models fumble it (Muse's one `plan!`-EDN event inlined "— expect:" into titles while the markdown path parsed cleanly).
- **Targets = the turn's ok eval rows** (`:seon.eval/ok? true`), failed forms excluded for free — the db already knows. Derive-don't-store applied to training data (stale exports re-derived, never patched; projection-sha stamped).
- **Serving on the src-diffusion wire contract** (`POST /run` + `/runsync`, `GET /status/{id}`, `/health` with `worker_sha`, in-band `gen_error`) — buys `bin/seon` supervision, idle-unload, sha-verification, URL-swap. `/runsync` verified present (server.py:356-369).
- **Measurement inside the three surfaces** (trainer-internal metrics in `src-needle`; the gate + agent-level lift as an inspect-ai task). No fourth harness.
- **MLX for inference and finetune; tok/s reported.** A1's exporter and the eventual port are kill-test *enablers*, not wasted work — but see ordering in §6.

## 3. What changes

Each: what → why → the concrete edit.

1. **Invert the data recipe (measured-fatal).** The will-it-work judge live-queried both stores: 260 turns ever / 224 with ok evals, all acme; default = 0 (reset wiped `store/`; blobs orphaned); per-kind: `db/transact!` 4, `reconcile!` 5, `defn` 21, `register!` 22, `query` ~58. "Mined turns" cannot be the primary source. → **Edit design.md §Data sources:** primary = synthetic/gold (frontier-driven sessions on ephemeral clusters generating (projection, forms) pairs at scale + orchestrator gold with agy paraphrase augmentation); secondary = forward-mined live turns as they accumulate; the existing ~224 mined turns become the **held-out real-distribution eval set, never training data**. **Edit CLAUDE.md Runbook:** delete the "stores to mine: acme (3.7G), default (545M)" line — it counted gigabytes of history/embeddings, not turns.

2. **Fix the plan training target (silent no-op / destructive-shape risk).** As written, `(context+markdown) → reconcile!{::markdown …}` trains a copy-wrap that `parse-markdown` (internal.cljs:661-714) already does deterministically, AND supervises whole-document reconcile as the reflex — the drive-evidenced destructive shape (`:dropped 2`, planner-worker-design.md:230-239; `:dropped 1`, :453-459). → **Edit design.md §The target:** primary plan target = shape selection + **resolved delta forms** (`step!`/`needs!`/`active!`/`move!`/`done!` with live `:my.plan/id`s present in the rendered `:plan` block — the byte-exact rule is what puts those ids in the encoder input); `reconcile!`-with-markdown is the target ONLY for authoring against an empty/near-empty tree. Add dataset-composition quotas: non-empty trees, incremental asks, id-resolution cases, ordering-bearing lists. Full recipe in §5.

3. **Kill the contrastive head as retriever (the one real ONE-mechanism violation).** The head is a within-example re-ranker trained with in-batch negatives over each example's own tool list (eval.py:514-529, dataset.py:593-620) — never validated as a corpus retriever; and the roadmap's "cache card embeddings — content-addressed" floats a second persisted index next to `seon.embed`/Proximum, banned by the ONE-mechanism table. All three judges converge. → **Edit design.md §Why needle fits:** delete "the CLIP-style contrastive head is the retrieval half." v0 card selection = deterministic (current-ns fns, plan-step-referenced symbols, registered attrs of entities in the projection) with optional semantic top-up via the **existing** `seon.embed` stack; cards feed the encoder only. **Edit roadmap open questions:** replace the embedding-cache line with an explicit owner-ruling request — any persisted needle-embedding index must not land silently. **Edit CLAUDE.md settled list** ("contrastive head does retrieval" is currently written as settled — it isn't).

4. **Add the missing §Model roles (owner direction #2 is absent from the design).** The mechanism is ~90% built and just needs naming — and the design's silence invites the one wrong move (registering needle in `provider-locality`, making a 26M encoder selectable as a chat provider and colliding on `SEON_DG_ENDPOINT`). → **Add a "Model roles" section to design.md** with the exact rows in §4 below, explicitly forbidding needle-as-provider.

5. **Redesign `:suggest` as call-once-then-derive (judge disagreement, resolved).** The duplication judge left "where the network call sits" as an open A1/B3 question; the integration judge showed the design is self-contradictory: a network call is not a derivation, and an **unstored** model output breaks as-of re-render determinism (the design's own foundation) AND makes B3 uptake unmeasurable if prompt-blob capture is dialed off. **I side with the integration judge.** A model output is a runtime artifact — the same sanctioned exception class as an LLM reply. → **Edit design.md §Serving:** a pre-render step calls the needle server via `/runsync` (bounded timeout, single attempt, NO retry — fail-soft is absence, not `call-llm!` authority), transacts the suggestion as a turn-scoped datom (+ blob for the text); the `:suggest` block is then PURE over that stored row — absent row ⇒ renders nothing. Delete "nothing stored, nothing to clear" for this block. Bonus: every subsequently-mined turn carries a free suggestion-contamination stamp for the exporter to filter.

6. **Fix the renderer name and spec the profile mechanically.** design.md:55 and CLAUDE.md name `seon.ctx/render-context` — no such CLJS namespace exists; the ONE producer is **`seon.agent.ctx/render-context`** (ctx.cljs:2468; `src/seon/ctx.clj` is the paused JVM track). And the promised profile is not expressible today — `::render-context-request` is `{id, db?}` only. → **Edit both docs**; spec: ONE new optional key `:seon.agent.ctx/profile` (list of block-name + per-block patch maps; present ⇒ filter stored children to listed names, excluding derived auto-run blocks, merge patches upsert-by-name; absent ⇒ byte-parity), the profile definition itself stored config→DB (e.g. `:seon.config/context-profiles`) so as-of exports regenerate under the profile in force at that t. The profile excludes `:suggest` by construction. **Coordinate with Track A**, which is editing this exact surface uncommitted right now (per-block `:token-cap`/`:cap-keep`, transcript `::readline?` — both node-first, both usable by the profile).

7. **Close the byte-exact determinism holes inside A1, before the exporter mines anything.** Verified holes: (a) `result/<id>` handle rendering depends on live process identity (transcript.cljs:525) — export-after-restart ≠ inference bytes on the recent tail; (b) `:warnings` uses a wall-clock 1-hour cutoff (warn.cljs:872) + live-var lookups (:951); (c) cache-breakpoint/escape-clipping read `@db/*conn*`, not the passed db (ctx.cljs:408-445). → **Add to A1's acceptance list:** a render-handles dial or profile-level strip (both sides); exclude or db-purify warnings for the profile; profile renders read the PASSED db.

8. **Decide target granularity NOW — it decides whether owner #3 is reachable.** The no-FFN envelope (needle's own doc: match/extract/copy, no per-position computation) covers transact-map assembly (~80% copy), plan transcription (~80%), `register!` (~70%); it does NOT cover `db/query` join graphs (~45%) or `defn` bodies (~25%) as whole-form targets — but as **bounded continuations** (next attr keyword, next form frame) even those contexts are high-copy. → **Edit design.md §The target:** whole-form targets for plan/transact/register; bounded continuations OR explicit v0 exclusion for query `:where` graphs and defn bodies, routed to the frontier/worker (the routing already exists). "General at every turn" stays the serving surface's north star, not the v0 model's contract. The exporter format depends on this — decide before A2 gold authoring.

9. **Reorder the roadmap — kill tests before the port.** KT1 needs only sentencepiece, no port, hours; it's currently buried "in B1" after the investment it should gate. → **Edit roadmap Ordered path** per §6, with kill thresholds written in.

10. **Serving hygiene.** Own env var `SEON_NEEDLE_ENDPOINT` seeded once into `:seon.config/suggest` datoms — never overload `SEON_DG_ENDPOINT` (needle can't serve `mode=step`; runtime failure). 11. **Harden the B3 ship gate.** No frontier with-vs-without-suggestion-text arm exists anywhere in prior evidence (arm3 ≈ arm1 for the diffusion driver is the nearest, and it's a null). → **Edit design.md §Measurement:** add a $0 baseline arm (cursor oracle `op:"cursor"` ~3-5ms + menu-derived deterministic suggestions) that must LOSE on the same gate (parses ∧ evals-clean ∧ matches) before the 26M model earns its server; adopt the P6 dead-weight kill criterion (uptake ≈ 0 with no outcome delta ⇒ the section dies); include the frontier with/without arm. ## 5. The plan-domain pipeline

The settled recipe for owner #1 — `(context → frontier markdown → mapping into existing my.plan forms)`, no new plan features:

1. **Context:** the autocomplete profile over `seon.agent.ctx/render-context` at the turn's db value — critically including the live `:plan` block, because the `:my.plan/id`s the model must emit are IN the encoder input. This is the byte-exact rule paying for itself.
2. **Frontier plan:** the planner (derived, §4) emits markdown. Two dialects matter: *structured* (`[id]`-tagged, `— expect:`-marked, explicitly nested — promptable) and *loose/incremental* prose.
3. **Training target = shape selection + resolved forms**, situation-conditioned:
   - **Fresh authoring against an empty/near-empty tree** → `reconcile!` with the markdown heredoc (authoring IS reconcile-against-empty; no identity to resolve, no drop hazard). This is the ONLY situation where the copy-wrap target is right.
   - **Incremental asks / non-empty trees** → resolved delta forms: `step!`/`needs!`/`active!`/`move!` with real live ids; genuine re-plans → `reconcile!` with ids preserved from the rendered document. This is where the trained model earns its existence — the parser's exact ceiling is line syntax within the document; judgment begins wherever the mapping needs the live tree (identity, what's open) or semantics the syntax doesn't carry (numbered-list ordering → `::needs`/`:after`, expect distillation from prose the parser silently discards, delta-vs-whole-document shape choice, `[x]`-claims never becoming `done!`).
4. **Where reconcile!-as-cheap-supervision applies — narrowly:** (a) as a *label source* ONLY for structured frontier markdown where the parse is information-preserving (prompt the planner into that shape and the parse IS the label); mechanically compiling *loose* markdown teaches ordering erasure, id-destruction-as-gold (the live `:dropped 2` failure becomes a supervised target), and whole-document-shape bias. (b) Always as the **oracle/filter**: run every candidate label through parse+compile and the diff receipt (`::added/::dropped/::updated`) + eval envelope; an unexpected `:dropped` count auto-rejects the label. (c) The genuinely judgment-bearing labels come from live frontier resolved-form turns (Muse's W1 trees, the escalation drive's diff-0/2/1 reconcile) — the pairs the cheap pipeline cannot manufacture.
5. **Dataset quotas (mandatory, or the model learns a no-op):** deliberate proportions of incremental-asks-against-non-empty-trees, id-resolution cases, and ordering-bearing lists. The dataset design decision matters more than the model.

## 6. Ordered kill-tests

Cheapest first; each gates the spend after it. (Judge disagreement resolved: I adopt the will-it-work judge's expanded six-test list over the other judges' three — each added test is near-free and kills a distinct unmeasured premise. KT1/KT2 gate the B1 port; KT3/KT4 gate training spend; KT5 gates full training.)

- **KT0 — corpus census. FREE — ALREADY RUN BY THIS REVIEW; FIRED.** 224 ok-eval turns (all acme), 0 in default; transact! 4, reconcile! 5, defn 21, register! 22, query ~58 vs ≥120-per-kind guidance. Consequence executed as change #1: data recipe inverted; mined turns = held-out eval only.
- **KT1 — tokenizer envelope (hours; sentencepiece only, no port, no GPU).** Encode ~200 real (profile-projection, ok-forms) pairs with the stock `needle.model`. **KILL "keep the tokenizer" — and with it, realistically, the checkpoint (tied embeddings make retokenize ≡ pretrain ≡ infeasible locally) — if >~25% of projections+cards exceed 1024 enc tokens, OR median target >512 dec tokens, OR byte-fallback fraction on targets >~35%.** Also converts the design's "~700 BPE" claim into a measured number. Runs BEFORE B1.
- **KT2 — zero-shot copy fidelity (hours; stock checkpoint inference).** 50 tool-call-shaped probes whose copyable value is Clojure-shaped (namespaced keyword, kebab-case, contains `/`); read the existing `value_acc`. **KILL the retarget if Clojure-value accuracy <~50% while English-value accuracy stays high** — the vehicle can't copy our identifiers through byte-fallback; assembly fails before logic enters. Gates committing to B1.
- **KT3 — frontier signal-ceiling on the projection (hours; ~$1-5).** Give DeepSeek/agy the EXACT profile projection + cards for the 224 held-out turns; score with the design's own gate. **KILL/fix-the-projection if the frontier model can't reach ~30-40% useful-match** — if a frontier model can't do it from this projection, no 26M model can; the defect is the projection, not the trainee.
- **KT4 — oracle-injection uptake (~1 day; modest spend; no trained model).** In src-inspect-ai, inject each turn's ACTUAL gold forms as the `:suggest` section for a frontier-driven task set vs no-section control; measure uptake + outcome delta. **KILL B3 serving (keep the dataset) if even perfect suggestions produce ~zero uptake/outcome change** — given the arm3≈arm1 null prior, the channel itself is unproven; this is the cheapest way to find out before any training.
- **KT5 — finetune reachability on the easiest kind (~1-2 days, local MLX).** 300-500 markdown-plan→resolved-my.plan-forms examples through the stock finetune loop (uniform loss, constrained decoding OFF); held-out exact-match. **KILL if held-out stays <~40-50% after train exact-match >90%** — if the highest-copy mapping doesn't generalize, query/defn are hopeless. PASS validates scoping v0 to plan+transact/register.
- **B3 ship gate (post-training):** the $0 baseline (cursor oracle + menu-derived suggestions) must LOSE on the identical gate, AND the P6 dead-weight criterion must not fire (uptake ≈ 0 with no outcome delta ⇒ the section dies), on a frontier with/without arm.

## 7. Honest risk register

1. **Tokenizer trap (existential):** byte-fallback inflation may blow the 1024/512 envelope, and the "retrain" fallback destroys the checkpoint (tied embeddings ⇒ pretrain ⇒ infeasible locally) — KT1 decides whether this vehicle exists at all; everything else is downstream.
2. **Channel value unproven:** the nearest measured analog is a null (inert menu text ≈ no menus, .267 vs .286); a *perfect* suggestion model could still be dead render weight plus every-turn latency — KT4 must fire before training spend.
3. **Data scarcity + distribution mismatch:** 224 real turns total; the inverted synthetic/gold-primary recipe is itself unvalidated (synthetic sessions may not match live turn distribution) — the mined held-out eval set is the only guard.
4. **Envelope vs owner scope:** query joins and defn bodies are outside the no-FFN copy envelope as whole forms; owner #3's "general autocomplete" is reachable only via bounded continuations or honest routing of those kinds to the frontier/worker — the per-form-kind gate will confine v0 to the copy-heavy kinds.
5. **Byte-exact drift in practice:** two verified determinism holes (process-identity result handles, warnings wall-clock) plus Track A editing the same surface uncommitted right now — every training pair minted before these close inside A1 is unreproducible from day one.
