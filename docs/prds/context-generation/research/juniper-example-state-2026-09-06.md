## Current composition finding

`src/seon/render/walk.clj:86` enumerates installed refs in both directions.
`acquisition-members` follows incoming plan/message refs; `shallow-entity`
removes reverse collections from the root render value and renders neighbors
separately. The agent's AI renderer selects its situation summary; HTML
selects its session history. Neither composes `my.plan/plan` with incoming
messages as the requested two logical blocks.

This is evidence about today's implementation, not a ruling to retain the
model. Next experiment: show a useful current-plan block with a real update
form, a received-message block, and the exact concatenated AI outputs.
Do not present the fixture as an actual provider prompt.
