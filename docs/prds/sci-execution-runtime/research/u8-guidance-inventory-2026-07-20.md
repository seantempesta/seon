# U8 guidance inventory — 2026-07-20

## Scope and timing ruling

This is the inventory-only pass for U8. The live agents still execute on Bun children, so their current `^:async`/`await` idiom remains correct and **must not be rewritten before cutover**. The future host sci tier instead receives synchronous database-call values. The dispositions below therefore describe cutover work; they do not authorize a current guidance edit.

The sweep was restricted to the requested authorities:

- agent-rendered docstrings in `src/seon/*.cljs` and `src/my/*.cljs`;
- `seon-skills/*/SKILL.md` and `.agents/skills/*/SKILL.md`;
- `:seon.warn/example` strings in `src/seon/warn.cljs`;
- `config/system.edn`;
- `docs/seon/architecture/toolkit.md` and `agent-runtime.md`.

The minimum patterns were `await`, `\.then`, `js/Promise`, and `\^:async`. Generated `.claude/skills` was deliberately excluded. Raw implementation forms/comments in CLJS were excluded unless they were an agent-rendered docstring or a warn example. `toolkit.md` had zero hits.

A “hit” below is one matching source line. A line containing more than one pattern is one row, which keeps the file:line inventory unambiguous.
